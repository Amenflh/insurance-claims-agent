# HomeShield Buildings & Contents — General Conditions

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.** Written for a demo project. It does not reproduce the conditions of any real insurer.
> Product: `home` · Market: international (UAE, France) · Language: English · Currency: as per schedule (AED, EUR)

## Art. 1 — Definitions
<!-- id: HOME-01 | kind: definition | keywords: definitions, insured, home, buildings, contents, schedule, définitions, habitation -->
"You" means the policyholder named in the schedule and members of the household living with them. "Home" means the
private dwelling at the address shown in the schedule. "Contents" means household goods and personal belongings.

## Art. 2 — Period of insurance
<!-- id: HOME-02 | kind: condition | check: policy_period | keywords: period of insurance, start date, expiry, renewal, période, échéance -->
Cover applies only to events occurring during the period of insurance shown in the schedule.

## Art. 3 — Fire and smoke
<!-- id: HOME-03 | kind: coverage | perils: home_fire | keywords: fire, smoke, explosion, lightning, ignited, burnt, incendie, fumée, feu -->
We cover loss of or damage to the buildings and contents caused by fire, smoke, explosion or lightning.

## Art. 4 — Escape of water
<!-- id: HOME-04 | kind: coverage | perils: home_water | keywords: escape of water, burst pipe, leak, flooded, washing machine, hose, ceiling, dégât des eaux, fuite, canalisation -->
We cover damage caused by water suddenly escaping from a fixed water installation, pipe, tank or domestic appliance,
including water coming from a neighbouring property.

## Art. 5 — Theft and burglary
<!-- id: HOME-05 | kind: coverage | perils: home_theft | keywords: theft, burglary, burgled, stolen, forced entry, break-in, vol, cambriolage, effraction -->
We cover theft of contents following forcible and violent entry into or exit from the home, and the damage caused to
the buildings by the break-in. The theft must be reported to the police.

## Art. 6 — Storm, hail and natural events
<!-- id: HOME-06 | kind: coverage | perils: home_storm | keywords: storm, hail, hailstorm, wind, roof, flood, tempête, grêle, toiture, vent -->
We cover damage caused by storm, hail, high winds, flood or other natural events of abnormal intensity.

## Art. 7 — Alternative accommodation
<!-- id: HOME-07 | kind: definition | keywords: alternative accommodation, hotel, uninhabitable, relogement -->
If the home becomes uninhabitable after an insured event, we pay reasonable alternative accommodation costs for up to
six months.

## Art. 8 — Exclusion: gradual damage and lack of maintenance
<!-- id: HOME-08 | kind: exclusion | applies_to: * | triggers: slow leak, gradual, for several months, for months, rotten, rust, corrosion, lack of maintenance, wear and tear, fuite lente, depuis des mois, défaut d'entretien | keywords: gradual deterioration, maintenance, wear, rot, usure, entretien -->
We do not cover damage that happens gradually over a period of time, including slow leaks, rot, rust, corrosion, wear
and tear, or damage resulting from a lack of maintenance.

## Art. 9 — Exclusion: unoccupied home
<!-- id: HOME-09 | kind: exclusion | applies_to: home_water, home_theft, home_fire | triggers: unoccupied, vacant, inoccupé, inoccupée, inoccupation | keywords: unoccupied, vacancy, inoccupation -->
We do not cover escape of water, theft or malicious damage while the home has been unoccupied for more than 60
consecutive days.

## Art. 10 — Exclusion: theft without forced entry
<!-- id: HOME-10 | kind: exclusion | applies_to: home_theft | triggers: no sign of forced entry, no forced entry, left unlocked, door was open, sans effraction, porte ouverte, porte non verrouillée | keywords: forced entry, unlocked, effraction -->
We do not cover theft where there is no evidence of forcible and violent entry, for example when a door or window was
left open or unlocked.

## Art. 11 — Exclusion: pets, vermin and mould
<!-- id: HOME-11 | kind: exclusion | applies_to: * | triggers: dog, cat, pet, chewed, rodents, rats, termites, mould, mold, chien, chat, rongeurs, moisissure | keywords: pets, animals, vermin, insects, mould, animaux -->
We do not cover damage caused by domestic pets, insects, vermin, rodents, mould or fungus.

## Art. 12 — Exclusion: deliberate acts
<!-- id: HOME-12 | kind: exclusion | applies_to: * | triggers: deliberately damaged, on purpose, intentionally damaged, volontairement | keywords: deliberate, intentional, malicious by the insured -->
We do not cover loss or damage caused deliberately by you or a member of your household.

## Art. 13 — Claims notification
<!-- id: HOME-13 | kind: condition | check: report_deadline | days: 7 | keywords: notification, report the claim, deadline, déclaration, délai -->
You must notify us within 7 days of discovering the damage. Late notifications are reviewed by a claims handler.

## Art. 14 — Large losses
<!-- id: HOME-14 | kind: condition | check: review_threshold | keywords: loss adjuster, large loss, threshold, expertise -->
Claims above the authority limit shown in the schedule are assessed by a loss adjuster before settlement.
